Privacy Policy for MyPostiz23

Last updated: June 1, 2026

Thank you for choosing MyPostiz23. Your privacy is critically important to us. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and protect your information when you connect and automate your social media channels (such as TikTok) via our application.

1. Data We Do Not Collect

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. All scheduling actions and channel integrations you authorize are managed under your direct control.

2. TikTok API Scope Usage

  • Basic Profile Info (user.info.basic, user.info.profile): Used solely to associate and display your authorized TikTok handle and avatar on your MyPostiz23 analytics dashboard.
  • Content Posting (video.create, video.publish, video.upload): Used solely to upload and publish the video files and captions you explicitly queue via our scheduling interface.

3. Data Protection

All authentication tokens are encrypted and securely stored in your dedicated database instance. We restrict third-party access to the maximum possible extent.

If you have any questions, please contact the administrator of your MyPostiz23 host system.
